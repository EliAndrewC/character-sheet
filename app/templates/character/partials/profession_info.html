Profession

No School Ring, no school knacks, no Dan

Abilities cost no XP. You get your first at 150 XP and one more every 15 XP after that, and you may mix them freely across professions - a wave man who settles in a city picks up a worker's habits, and one who prays often enough is taught a ritual. You can still buy school knacks from other schools below.

Most abilities may be taken twice. Priest rituals may be taken only once - a ritual is something you have been taught, not a knack you deepen.
