# Roll statistics (averages and probability charts) - Design

Status: **built** (2026-10-17).

## 1. Goal

Some players love the probability side of the game and some do not. A **per-player setting, off by default**, shows:

- **an average next to every roll** on the character sheet (skills, knacks, rings, athletics, attack, parry, wound check);
- **a click on that average opens a statistics modal**: a bar chart of how often each range of totals comes up, the average and how it breaks down (dice, school abilities, flat bonuses), and a **void stepper** that redraws everything for 0, 1, 2, ... void points. The modal rolls nothing.
- **hovering (or tapping) a bar** shows that range's chance and, for abilities whose bonus varies, how much of a total in that range is the dice and how much the ability (a Shosuro 5th Dan roll of 50-54: dice ~38, lowest three dice ~12).

## 2. Decisions

| # | decision |
|---|---|
| P1 | **Per viewer, not per character**: `User.preferences["roll_stats_enabled"]`, a checkbox in Profile > Roll Settings, off by default. Anonymous visitors never see it. (GM, 2026-10-17: "disabled by default ... in your personal settings".) |
| P2 | **Every roll** with a single total gets an average: skills, knacks, rings, athletics, attack (every variant), parry, wound check. Not initiative (action dice, not a total) and not damage (its dice depend on how far the attack beat its TN, which the damage modal already shows). |
| P3 | **Post-roll flat bonuses are left out of the chart and the average** (3rd Dan free raises, Conviction, Courtier / Doji 5th Dan, alternative totals): they move the curve without changing its shape. The modal lists what is available as **one-line notes** under the chart. |
| P4 | **Shape-changing abilities are modelled**: Shosuro Actor 5th Dan (automatic), Hida Bushi 3rd Dan and Merchant 5th Dan (rerolls the player chooses, under a stated policy, see P6), and a **"keep the better of two rolls"** toggle for Lucky / a PCP reroll / the Merchant's business reroll. |
| P5 | **Bins are 5 wide and aligned on multiples of 5** (20-24, 25-29, ...), since TNs are multiples of 5: "the chance to reach TN 25" is the sum of the bars from 25 up. |
| P6 | **Reroll policies.** Hida 3rd Dan (each chosen die is replaced, as the server does; the inventory says the better is kept - flagged to the GM, see `school-features/HidaBushi.md`): the sheet's own pre-selection - the lowest dice showing 7 or less, up to N (X attack skill; 2X on a counterattack; halved, rounded up, when Impaired), each replaced by a fresh exploding die, as `roll_sessions._hida_reroll` does. Merchant 5th Dan: the set of dice the rule allows that most raises the expected sum of all the dice (dropped dice included: a rerolled low die often lands among the kept) (`merchant_choice` in the generator). The keep-better toggle is the max of two independent rolls, i.e. "I always reroll and keep the higher": an upper bound on what a reroll is worth. |
| P7 | **The server computes everything** (`app/services/roll_stats.py`); the browser only draws. `GET /characters/{id}/roll-stats?key=<roll key>` answers every void level and both toggle states for one roll, lazily when the modal opens, so the sheet does not carry distributions it may never show. Averages for the badges come rendered with the page and are re-fetched (`/roll-stats/averages`) when wounds change, since Impaired changes the 10s rule. |
| P8 | **Pre-computed tables, no Monte Carlo at request time.** Ordinary rolls use `app/data/probabilities.py` (a survival function per rolled / kept / reroll cell: the chance of each total is one subtraction). The three shape-changers use `app/data/roll_shapes.json`, generated by `scripts/generate_roll_shapes.py` (200k trials per cell, fixed seed): per cell, the chance of each total and the mean ability part at each total. |
| P9 | **A void point is applied exactly as a real roll applies it** (`roll_engine.formula_with_void`, shared with `execute_roll`): +1k1, the 10k10 ceiling with +2 per kept die above it, Mirumoto 5th Dan's +10 on combat rolls. The stepper runs from 0 to the per-roll cap less any activation point (Commune); 0 only when void is blocked (Discordant). It is hypothetical: it ignores how many points the character holds right now. |
| P10 | **The total cap** (Withdrawn's "never higher than 15") folds every total above it into the cap, so the chart has nothing above it. |
| P11 | **Badge placement**: beside the formula on skills, attack, parry, knacks and the wound check, and inside the ring circles. At phone width the ring circles (72px) have no room, so their badges hide there; the modal's roll picker lists every roll, athletics rings and variants included. |

| P12 | **The attack and wound-check modals keep their own hit / wound tables.** Those answer "against this TN, right now"; the statistics modal answers "what does this roll look like". They coexist. |

### Round 2 (2026-10-17)

| # | decision |
|---|---|
| P13 | **Whole numbers** for every average shown (badges and the modal); the server keeps tenths. |
| P14 | **The void row always shows.** Where the rules allow no void it shows 0 and says why (Discordant, the iaijutsu strike, the Xk1 roll's price, an activation point that uses the whole limit). |
| P15 | **The 3rd Dan Xk1 rolls** (Isawa Ishi add, Ide subtract) get a badge and statistics, from the formula their button rolls. |
| P16 | **A generic `/probabilities` page**, public: "Average by dice" (rolled down the side, kept across) and "Chance to reach a TN" (one row per XkY, TNs 5-100), each with or without rerolling 10s, chosen in the URL (`?chart=tn&reroll=0`). Any cell or row opens the same chart as the sheet's modal (shared `_roll_chart.html` + `static/js/roll_chart.js`). Below 1% shows tenths and is dimmed. |
| P17 | **Links to it**: every roll modal's "⋮" menu (now always present, PCP options below when they apply; the parry, feint and Kakita 5th Dan modals gained one), the roll history page, the statistics modal and the site footer, each opening a new tab except the footer. The page points to the profile checkbox (`/profile#roll-statistics`, highlighted when targeted). |

| P18 | **A bar's dice / bonus split shows only when an ability's share varies by bar** (Shosuro 5th Dan, the Hida and Merchant rerolls). Otherwise the dice are just the range less the flat bonuses, which tells the reader nothing (GM, 2026-10-17). |
| P19 | **One kept die with 10s rerolled gets a footnote** explaining the dip: the dice never total exactly 10, 20, ..., so 10-14 has four ways to happen and 15-19 five. Only then: with two or more kept dice the gaps fill in. |

### Not modelled (said so in the modal where it applies)

- **Wave Man W5** (one freed 10 while Impaired): the chart uses the no-reroll table. Rare (an Impaired Wave Man).
- **The Merchant's post-roll void** and other post-roll dice: like P3.
- Post-roll bonuses that depend on state (Akodo / Bayushi / Matsu banked amounts, attack specializations) are not in the notes yet.
- Which way a Lucky reroll interacts with a Hida / Merchant reroll on the same roll: the toggle takes two independent rolls of the ability-included distribution.
